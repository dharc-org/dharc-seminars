Why Ollama, and not LM Studio?

The notebook talks to Ollama (at http://localhost:11434) and only sees the models installed in Ollama. Models downloaded with LM Studio, or as files from Hugging Face, are invisible to it. If you already use LM Studio, keep it, but install Ollama too for the workshop.
